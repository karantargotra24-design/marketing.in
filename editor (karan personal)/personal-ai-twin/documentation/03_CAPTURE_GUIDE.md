# What to record (about 20 min, phone only)

**Setup:** window light in front of you, plain wall, phone at eye level, 4K/1080p, no beauty filter, the same outfit you'll use in videos (solid black, no logos). Quiet room for audio, phone 30 cm away, no music.

1. **Photos (real camera, no filters):** front neutral ×3, 45° left ×3, 45° right ×3, 90° left ×2, 90° right ×2, chin slightly up ×2, chin slightly down ×2, smile ×3, serious ×3.
2. **Talking-head video:** 3–5 min single take, facing the camera, speaking naturally in Hinglish about your work. Pause 1 s between sentences, close your mouth fully at pauses, use natural hand gestures inside the frame.
3. **Voice:** 3+ min of clean speech (instant clone). For the best quality, 30+ min across several sessions: read scripts and speak freely, in Hinglish and English.
4. Share via Drive "02 Real Photos" → a "twin-raw" subfolder. Don't send them through git.
