FREE CHECKLIST

Clicks but zero leads?LEAD-LEAK CHECKLIST

Go through this on your own ad before you spend more. Tick every box.

01Leak 1: Slow or confusing page

  • Open the page on mobile data, not Wi-Fi
  • The offer is clear at first glance, above the fold
  • One main button, and it matches the ad's promise
  • Price or price range in ₹ is visible (or clearly 'on call')
  • Phone number / WhatsApp works when tapped

02Leak 2: Too many form fields

  • Only ask what you need for the first call (often name + phone)
  • No compulsory fields you never use
  • Form works on mobile and shows a thank-you message
  • You get notified the moment a form is filled

03Leak 3: Slow replies

  • Instant auto-reply on WhatsApp / DM / form
  • A real person follows up the same day
  • 3-message follow-up plan: right away, next day, day three
  • Every lead is logged in one sheet or CRM

04Leak 4: A vague offer

  • Says what they get, for whom and the price range
  • One clear reason to act now (real, not fake urgency)
  • Proof is real: reviews, photos, results you can show
  • Ad, page and reply all say the same thing
Rule: fix the leaks first, then raise the budget. More budget on a leaky funnel buys more of the same problem.
