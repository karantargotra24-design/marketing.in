# weekly-intelligence

**Purpose:** Weekly research brief with primary sources and a 0-100 score per idea.

**Required inputs:** niche, current week

**Workflow:** 1 Search current week. 2 Primary sources only for facts. 3 Score each idea out of 100 (relevance, timeliness, proof, brand fit). 4 Top 5.

**Output template:** research/weekly-<date>.md

**Quality checklist:** Dated sources; scores explained. Plus rules in knowledge/00 (sources, no invented data, isolation).

**Example invocation:** /weekly-intel
