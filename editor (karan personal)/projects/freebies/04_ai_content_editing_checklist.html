Using AI for content?EDITING CHECKLIST

Run every AI-assisted post through these 11 checks before you publish.

01Make it yours

  • Add one real example from your own work
  • Rewrite the opening line the way you'd say it out loud
  • Cut words you'd never use ("unlock", "game-changer", "dive in")
  • Keep one idea per post

02Make it true

  • Check every number, date and tool feature against a real source
  • Remove any result, client or story that didn't happen
  • Mark AI-generated images where required

03Make it work

  • The first line says the benefit
  • The hook is different from your last few posts
  • One clear call to action
  • Read it on your phone before posting
Remember: AI is your first draft, not your final post.
