## C2 · Ads getting clicks but no leads: 4 fixes

1. **Getting clicks but zero leads?** · 4 fixes to check before you raise your budget
2. **More budget won't fix** [a leaky funnel] · If people click but don't enquire, the problem is usually after the click. Fix these 4 things first, then scale.
3. **Fix 1: The page is slow** [or confusing] · Open your landing page on mobile data. If the offer isn't clear at first glance, rewrite the top section.
4. **Fix 2: Too many form fields** [ask less] · Name and phone are often enough to start. Ask the rest on the call. Each extra field adds friction.
5. **Fix 3: Slow replies** [reply fast] · A lead who waits hours may have moved on. Set up an instant reply on WhatsApp or DM, then follow up.
6. **Fix 4: A vague offer** [be specific] · 'Best services in town' says nothing. Say what they get, who it's for and the price range in ₹.
7. **Want my lead-leak checklist?** · Comment "AI" and I'll DM you the 4-point checklist.
