## Ground rules (say these in Class 1)
1. **AI drafts, you decide.** Always check facts, prices and claims.
2. **Never paste private client data** (phone lists, passwords, unpublished numbers) into AI tools.
3. **Disclose and be honest.** Don't create fake reviews, fake testimonials or fake people endorsing a product.
4. **Respect copyright and likeness.** Don't copy other brands' creatives or use real people's faces/voices without permission.

# CLASS 1: AI Tools Landscape + Prompt Engineering for Marketing
**Goal:** students understand what AI is good and bad at, and write prompts that produce usable marketing output.

### Run of show (120 min)
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Welcome, intros, ground rules |
| 10–25 | What AI is good at / bad at |
| 25–55 | The prompt formula |
| 55–80 | Live demo: from a weak to a strong prompt |
| 80–105 | Activity: build your brand brief + 5 core prompts |
| 105–120 | Quiz + homework |

### Teaching notes
**1. Good at:** first drafts, variations, summarising, rewriting tone, ideas, structuring, translation/Hinglish, formulas and spreadsheets.
**Bad at:** current facts (unless it can browse), your local market specifics, exact numbers, knowing your brand without being told. It can **confidently make things up**, so always verify.

**2. The prompt formula: R-C-T-F-E**
- **Role:** "You are a performance marketer for local businesses in Jammu, India."
- **Context:** business, audience, offer, tone, platform
- **Task:** exactly what to produce
- **Format:** table, bullets, word count, number of options
- **Examples:** paste 1–2 examples of what "good" looks like

**3. Weak vs strong**
- Weak: "Write an Instagram caption for my bakery."
- Strong:
```
You are a social media copywriter for local food brands in Jammu.
Business: Royal Bakes, custom cakes, Gandhi Nagar, delivery in 4 hrs, ₹650+ per kg.
Audience: 22–40, ordering for birthdays and anniversaries, mostly on WhatsApp.
Tone: warm, fun, a little Hinglish, no cringe.
Task: write 5 Instagram captions for a reel showing a custom photo cake being made.
Format: each caption = hook line + 2 short lines + CTA "WhatsApp to order (link in bio)". Max 40 words. No hashtags.
```

**4. The brand brief (the most valuable thing in this course)**
A 1-page document you paste at the start of every chat (or save as custom instructions / a project):
```
Brand: … | What we sell: … | Price range: …
Customer: who, age, area, problem, desire
Why us: 3 reasons | Proof: reviews, numbers (real only)
Tone: … | Words we use / never use: …
Offer right now: … | CTA: …
```

**5. Iterate, don't restart**
"Make hook 3 shorter." · "Rewrite for 45+ audience." · "Give 10 more like #2." · "Turn this into a 20-sec reel script."

### Live demo
Same task, three prompts: weak → medium → R-C-T-F-E with the brand brief. Compare outputs on screen.

### Activity
Each student writes their brand brief + saves 5 core prompts: captions, reel script, ad copy, content ideas, reply to a customer DM.

### Quiz: Class 1
1. What does R-C-T-F-E stand for?
   **Role, Context, Task, Format, Examples**
2. Name 2 things AI is bad at.
   **Current facts, exact numbers, local specifics, knowing your brand without context (any 2)**
3. Why create a brand brief?
   **So every output matches your business, audience and tone without re-explaining**
4. Should you paste a client's customer phone list into a chat tool?
   **No: private data**
5. The output is 80% good. Restart or iterate?
   **Iterate with specific edits**

### Homework
- Finish your brand brief + 5-prompt library (**Assignment 1**, due before Class 3).

# CLASS 2: Captions, Scripts & Ad Copy with AI
**Goal:** students produce a month of content ideas, reel scripts and ad copy in one sitting, in their brand voice.

### Run of show (120 min)
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Recap + 2 brand briefs reviewed |
| 10–35 | 30-day content plan in 15 minutes |
| 35–65 | Reel scripts that hold attention |
| 65–90 | Ad copy variations + hooks testing |
| 90–110 | Activity: produce your week |
| 110–120 | Quiz + homework |

### Teaching notes
**1. 30-day plan prompt**
```
[Paste brand brief]
Create a 30-day Instagram plan as a table: Day | Pillar (Educate/Proof/Behind the scenes/Offer) | Format (Reel/Carousel/Story) | Hook | Key message | CTA.
Rules: 3 value posts for every 1 offer post, at least 12 reels, local Jammu references where natural, no fake statistics.
```
Then: "Mark the 5 ideas most likely to get reach from non-followers and explain why."

**2. Reel script prompt**
```
Write a 20-second reel script for idea #[X].
Format: table with Time | On-screen text | What I say | B-roll shot.
Hook in the first 2 seconds. End with CTA "WhatsApp karo, link in bio".
Give 3 alternative hooks.
```

**3. Ad copy prompt**
```
[Brand brief]
Write 5 Meta ad variations for [offer]. Each uses a different angle: price, problem, proof, urgency (only if real), curiosity.
Format: Primary text (max 90 words, emoji bullets) | Headline (max 7 words) | CTA button.
Avoid: guaranteed results, personal attributes ("Are you overweight?"), fake scarcity.
```

**4. Make it sound human**
- Ask for "Hinglish the way people in Jammu actually text"
- Remove AI tells: "Unlock", "Elevate", "In today's fast-paced world", "Dive into"
- Read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it, rewrite it.

**5. Repurpose one idea into 5 pieces**
Reel → carousel → 3 stories → WhatsApp status → Google Business post.

### Live demo
From one brand brief, produce live: a 30-day plan, 3 reel scripts, 5 ad variations and 1 repurposing set, in under 25 minutes.

### Activity
Students produce **7 days of ready-to-post content** (scripts + captions) for their practice business.

### Quiz: Class 2
1. Name 3 "AI tell" words to delete.
   **Unlock, Elevate, Dive into, Fast-paced world, Seamless (any 3)**
2. What's the value-to-offer ratio rule?
   **3:1**
3. Why ask for 3 alternative hooks?
   **To test which opening stops the scroll**
4. What's one ad claim AI might write that you must remove?
   **Guaranteed results / fake urgency / personal-attribute wording**
5. One reel idea can become which other formats?
   **Carousel, stories, WhatsApp status, GBP post**

### Homework
- Post or schedule at least 2 AI-assisted pieces this week (screenshot results later).
- Collect 5–10 product/business photos for Class 3.

# CLASS 3: AI Images & Short Videos for Social and Ads
**Goal:** students create on-brand images, edit product photos, and make short AI-assisted videos with captions.

### Run of show (120 min)
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Recap + homework results |
| 10–35 | Image prompting that looks real, not "AI-ish" |
| 35–60 | Editing real photos: background removal, expand, clean-up |
| 60–90 | Video: CapCut auto-captions, AI voiceover, templates |
| 90–105 | AI video and avatars: what's possible + ethics |
| 105–120 | Quiz + homework (Assignment 2 brief) |

### Teaching notes
**1. Image prompt formula: Subject · Setting · Style · Lighting · Camera · Format**
```
Photorealistic close-up of a chocolate truffle birthday cake on a wooden table in a small bakery,
warm evening light, shallow depth of field, shot on a 50mm lens, 4:5 vertical, no text.
```
Then add text yourself in Canva; AI images often misspell text.

**2. Real > fake for local brands**
Use AI to **improve real photos** (clean background, extend for 9:16, fix lighting) rather than inventing products. Customers buy what they'll actually get. **Never generate fake "customers" or fake reviews.**

**3. Canva Magic tools (and similar)**
Background remover · Magic Eraser · Magic Expand (resize to 9:16 / 4:5) · Magic Write · Brand Kit (logo, colours, fonts) for consistency.

**4. Video workflow (CapCut)**
1. Shoot 5–8 short clips on your phone (5 sec each)
2. Auto-captions → fix spelling → brand font/colours
3. Add a hook text in the first frame
4. Music at low volume; voiceover with your own voice or AI text-to-speech (disclose if a client asks)
5. Export 9:16, 1080×1920

**5. AI video / avatars (demo)**
Text-to-video and avatar tools can make B-roll or explainer clips. Rules: use your **own** face/voice or licensed avatars only, label AI where it could mislead, and keep it for B-roll and explainers, not fake testimonials.

### Live demo
1. Turn 1 raw product photo into: a clean 1:1 post, a 9:16 story and a 4:5 ad (Canva)
2. Generate 2 lifestyle background images and composite the real product
3. Make a 15-sec reel in CapCut with auto-captions + hook text

### Quiz: Class 3
1. 6 parts of the image prompt formula:
   **Subject, Setting, Style, Lighting, Camera, Format**
2. Why add text in Canva instead of the AI image?
   **AI often misspells text in images**
3. Is it OK to generate "happy customer" photos for testimonials?
   **No: fake testimonials mislead buyers**
4. Export size for reels:
   **9:16, 1080×1920**
5. Which tool feature turns a square photo into a 9:16 story?
   **Magic Expand / AI outpainting**

### Homework
- **Assignment 2:** AI content pack (due before Class 4).

# CLASS 4: Automation: Leads to Sheets and WhatsApp
**Goal:** students build a working no-code automation: a form lead lands in Google Sheets, the owner gets an alert, and the lead gets a reply.

### Run of show (120 min)
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Recap + content pack highlights |
| 10–25 | Automation thinking: trigger → action |
| 25–70 | Live build: Google Form → Sheet → email alert → auto-reply |
| 70–90 | Meta lead form → Sheet (Zapier/Make/n8n); WhatsApp options |
| 90–105 | AI inside automations: summarise & classify leads |
| 105–120 | Final project brief (Assignment 3), quiz, wrap-up |

### Teaching notes
**1. Every automation = Trigger → (Filter) → Action(s)**
| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| New Google Form response | Add row to Sheet + email owner |
| New Meta lead | Add row to Sheet + WhatsApp/email alert |
| New row with status "Won" | Send thank-you + review link |
| Every Monday 9 AM | Email weekly lead summary |

**2. Build 1 (no paid tools): Google Form → Sheet → alert**
- Google Form responses go to a Sheet automatically
- In the Sheet: Extensions → Apps Script → `onFormSubmit` trigger → send an email to the owner (provide a ready script in the Drive folder)
- Or the Zapier/Make free tier: Google Forms → Gmail

**3. Build 2: Meta lead ads → Sheet**
Zapier/Make/n8n trigger "New lead (Facebook Lead Ads)" → action "Create spreadsheet row" → action "Send email/notification".

**4. WhatsApp options (be honest about limits)**
- **Free & manual:** the alert includes a `wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=…` link, so the owner taps once and the message is pre-written
- **WhatsApp Business app:** greeting/away messages + quick replies (no API needed)
- **WhatsApp Business Platform (API):** true auto-messages via providers (paid per conversation, needs approved templates). Teach the concept; set up only for real business needs.

**5. AI step inside the automation**
Add an AI action (ChatGPT/Claude via Zapier/Make/n8n) to:
- Classify the lead: Hot / Warm / Cold from the form answers
- Summarise long messages into 1 line
- Draft a personalised first reply for the owner to approve
Keep a human in the loop before anything is sent to customers.

**6. n8n note**
n8n is powerful and can be self-hosted; good for agencies. Zapier/Make are easier for beginners.

### Live demo
Build Build 1 end to end on screen, submit a test, show the Sheet row + email arrive. Then show a pre-built Build 2 with an AI classification step.

### Quiz: Class 4
1. Every automation has which 2 core parts?
   **Trigger and action**
2. Free way to message a lead on WhatsApp with one tap:
   **wa.me link with pre-filled text**
3. What's needed for true automatic WhatsApp messages?
   **WhatsApp Business Platform (API) via a provider + approved templates**
4. Name one useful AI step in a lead automation.
   **Classify hot/warm/cold, summarise, draft reply**
5. Why keep a human in the loop?
   **To check AI output before it reaches customers**

### Wrap-up
- Completion: all 4 classes + 3 assignments
- Next: **Meta Ads in Practice** to put the AI-made creatives into real campaigns
- Feedback form + ask for a review (screenshot or 30-sec video)
