# Typography Guidelines

## 1. Karan's personal brand — VERIFIED (Design System v2)
One editorial pairing for every format (Google Fonts, all free):

| Layer | Font | Weight | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Serif headline | **Instrument Serif** | Regular | Carries the sentence |
| Punch word | **Plus Jakarta Sans** | ExtraBold 800, tracking −0.04em, UPPERCASE or a number | Carries the payoff (one per headline) |
| Connector / kicker | **Instrument Serif Italic** | Regular, small (~0.42em of headline) | Linking words: "that make", "for", "is" |
| Body / labels | **Inter** | 400 / 500 / 600 | Body, footers, tags |

Sizes on a 1080px canvas:
| Format | Headline | Punch | Connector | Body |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Photo cover 4:5 / 9:16 | 150 | 170 | 56 | 30 (sub-line) |
| Type cover / Reel cover / slide 1 | 140 | 1.08em | .42em | 36 |
| Carousel step | title 88 + red serif number 240 | 88 | kicker 44 | 36 (≤35 words) |
| Story | 128 | 140 | 52 | 42 |
| Quote card | 96 | — | — | 28 attribution |

Rules: max 2 families + Inter, max 3 headline sizes per design, one punch word, one red element. Wordmark = "Karan Targotra" in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, −0.03em (no logo exists). Footer handle `@karantargotra.a`.
Note: artifact tokens.css still lists Space Grotesk / Inter for some sizes — out of date; README + components + Brand Kit say Instrument Serif / Plus Jakarta / Inter. Follow those.
