# image-video-generation-prompts

**Purpose:** Write generator prompts that respect brand.

**Required inputs:** approved brief

**Workflow:** 1 Subject, style, framing, text-safe zones. 2 Negative prompts. 3 Mark 'prompt only'.

**Output template:** prompts in briefs

**Quality checklist:** No real-person likeness without consent; no fake claims. Plus rules in knowledge/00 (sources, no invented data, isolation).

**Example invocation:** creative-director
