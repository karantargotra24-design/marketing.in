# CLASS 1: How Digital Marketing Actually Works
**Goal:** students understand the funnel and which channel does which job, and pick their practice business.

### Run of show (90 min)

| 50–65 | Live demo: reverse-engineer a local business |
| 65–80 | Activity: map your practice business |
| 80–90 | Q&A, quiz link, homework |

### Teaching notes
**1. Marketing = getting the right person to take the next step.**
Not "posting". Not "going viral". Every piece of marketing should move someone one step closer to buying.

**2. The funnel (draw it on screen):**
- **See (awareness):** they find out you exist (reels, ads, Google Maps)
- **Think (consideration):** they check you out (profile, reviews, website, highlights)
- **Do (conversion):** they enquire or buy (WhatsApp, call, form, visit)
- **Care (retention):** they come back and refer (follow-up, offers, community)

Jammu example: someone sees a gym reel → checks the profile and Google reviews → sends a WhatsApp message about fees → joins → renews and brings a friend.

**3. Channels and their jobs:**
| Channel | Best job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram / Facebook organic | Trust + awareness | Reels, posts, stories |
| Meta Ads | Reach new people fast + leads | Lead form / click-to-WhatsApp |
| Google Business Profile | Capture "near me" searches | "car service near me Gandhi Nagar" |
| Google Ads | Capture people already searching | "MBBS abroad consultant Jammu" |
| WhatsApp | Close and follow up | Reply, catalogue, reminders |
| Website / landing page | Explain + convert | Offer page with enquiry form |

**4. The 3 numbers every beginner must know:**
- **Reach:** how many people saw it
- **Enquiries (leads):** how many raised their hand
- **Cost per lead (CPL):** money spent ÷ leads

"Likes are applause. Leads are customers knocking."

# CLASS 2: Instagram & Facebook: Content That Sells
**Goal:** students can fix a profile, choose content pillars, and write posts and reels that lead to enquiries.

### Run of show (90 min)
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Recap + 2 student Class 1 sheets reviewed live |
| 10–25 | The profile that converts |
| 25–45 | Content pillars + formats |
| 45–60 | Hooks, captions and CTAs |
| 60–75 | Live demo: make a reel script + post in Canva |
| 75–90 | Activity + Q&A + homework |

### Teaching notes
**1. Profile = your shop window (fix this before posting more).**
- Profile photo: logo or clear face
- Name field: `Business | keyword`, e.g. "Royal Bakes | Cakes Jammu" (the name field is searchable)
- Bio: who you help + what you offer + location + CTA
  `Custom cakes in 4 hrs 🎂 | Gandhi Nagar, Jammu | Order on WhatsApp 👇`
- Link: WhatsApp link (`wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX`) or link page
- Highlights: Menu/Price · Reviews · Location · Offers
- Business account → contact buttons ON

**2. Content pillars: the 4-bucket system**
| Pillar | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Educate | Build authority | "3 mistakes when buying a used car" |
| Proof | Build trust | Reviews, before/after, deliveries, happy customers |
| Behind the scenes | Build connection | Making process, team, day in the life |
| Offer | Get enquiries | Price, offer, limited slots, "DM to book" |

Rule of thumb: **3 value posts : 1 offer post.**

**3. Formats**
- **Reels:** reach new people (most non-follower reach comes from here)
- **Carousels:** saves and education
- **Stories:** daily trust + sales (polls, questions, links)

**4. Hooks (first 2 seconds decide everything)**
- Problem: "Your cake order got cancelled last minute?"
- Curiosity: "Nobody in Jammu talks about this…"
- Number: "3 things to check before your car service"
- Result: "This 1 change doubled our enquiries"
- Local: "Jammu walo, ye dekho 👀" (Hinglish works for local audiences)

**5. Caption formula: Hook → Value → Proof → CTA**
CTA must be one clear action: "DM 'CAKE' to order" / "Tap WhatsApp in bio" / "Save this for later".

### Live demo (15 min)
In Canva, build one carousel (4 slides) + write one 20-sec reel script for a student's practice business, live, taking ideas from chat.

Reel script template:
```
0–2s  HOOK (text on screen + say it)
2–12s 3 quick points / visual proof
12–18s Result or reason to trust
18–20s CTA: "WhatsApp karo, link in bio"
```

### In-class activity
Write 3 hooks + 1 full caption for your practice business. Best 3 get reviewed live.

3. Recommended ratio of value posts to offer posts:
   a) 1:3 b) **3:1** c) 10:1
4. What should every caption end with?
   a) Hashtags b) **One clear CTA** c) Emojis
5. Name the 4 content pillars.
   **Educate, Proof, Behind the scenes, Offer**

# CLASS 3: Meta Ads Basics: Your First Campaign
**Goal:** students understand campaign structure, objectives, audiences and budgets, and plan a first campaign.

### Run of show (90 min)
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Recap + Assignment 1 highlights (anonymised) |
| 10–25 | Boost vs Ads Manager |
| 25–45 | Campaign → Ad set → Ad |
| 45–65 | Live demo: Ads Manager walkthrough (don't publish) |
| 65–80 | Budget maths + reading results |
| 80–90 | Q&A + homework |

### Teaching notes
**1. Boost button vs Ads Manager**
Boost is quick but limited (mostly engagement). Ads Manager gives the right objective, better targeting, lead forms, click-to-WhatsApp, and proper tracking. **For leads, use Ads Manager.**

**2. Structure (3 levels)**
- **Campaign:** the objective (what you want Meta to optimise for)
- **Ad set:** who (audience, location), where (placements), how much (budget, schedule)
- **Ad:** what they see (creative + text + CTA)

**3. Objectives (current Meta options):** Awareness · Traffic · Engagement · Leads · App promotion · Sales
For local businesses:
- **Leads** → instant forms (enquiries inside Facebook/Instagram)
- **Engagement / Leads with messaging** → click-to-WhatsApp
- **Awareness** → only for very local reach (new shop opening)

**4. Audience for local business**
- Location: city or radius around the shop (e.g. Jammu + 15 km)
- Age: based on the real buyer, not "everyone"
- Interests: optional. Broad targeting often works when the creative is clear.
- **The creative does the targeting.** "Jammu walo" + local visuals = local audience.

**5. Budget maths (₹)**
- Test: ₹300–₹500/day for 5–7 days
- Example: ₹400/day × 7 days = ₹2,800. If CPL = ₹70 → about 40 leads.
- Don't judge an ad in 24 hours; give it 3–4 days unless it is clearly broken.

**6. Reading results (the 5 columns to show)**
Amount spent · Results (leads) · Cost per result (CPL) · CTR (link) · Frequency
- CTR under ~1% → creative/hook problem
- Frequency over ~3 on a small audience → people are tired of the ad; refresh the creative

### Live demo
Ads Manager screen share: create a Leads campaign → ad set with Jammu + 15 km → placements → upload creative → instant form with 3 questions (name, phone, "what are you looking for?"). **Close without publishing**, or publish a ₹100 test on your own page.

### Activity
In pairs (breakout rooms) or solo: fill the campaign plan template for the practice business (this becomes Assignment 3).

### Quiz: Class 3
1. The 3 levels of a Meta campaign in order:
   **Campaign → Ad set → Ad**
2. Budget and audience are set at which level?
   a) Campaign b) **Ad set** c) Ad
3. Best objective to collect phone numbers inside Instagram:
   a) Traffic b) **Leads (instant form)** c) Awareness
4. ₹3,000 spent, 50 leads. CPL = ?
   **₹60**
5. Frequency is 5.2 and CPL is rising. What do you do first?
   **Refresh the creative / new ad** (audience is tired)

# CLASS 4: Google Business Profile & Local SEO
**Goal:** students can create and optimise a Google Business Profile and get found on "near me" searches.

### Run of show (90 min)
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Recap + content calendar feedback |
| 10–25 | Why Google Maps is free money for local businesses |
| 25–55 | Live demo: GBP optimisation checklist |
| 55–70 | Reviews system |
| 70–80 | Website basics (when you need one) |
| 80–90 | Quiz + homework |

### Teaching notes
**1. How local ranking works (the 3 factors Google talks about):**
**Relevance** (does the profile match the search?) · **Distance** (how close?) · **Prominence** (reviews, activity, reputation)

**2. GBP optimisation checklist**
- [ ] Correct business name (no keyword stuffing; it can get the profile suspended)
- [ ] Right **primary category** (the most important choice) + secondary categories
- [ ] Address/pin exact; service area if you visit customers
- [ ] Phone + WhatsApp + website link
- [ ] Hours incl. holidays
- [ ] 10+ real photos: outside, inside, team, products; add new ones monthly
- [ ] Services/products with prices where possible
- [ ] Description: what you do + areas served (Gandhi Nagar, Trikuta Nagar, Bahu Plaza…)
- [ ] Weekly post (offer, update, event)
- [ ] Q&A section answered

**3. Reviews system (the biggest lever)**
- Ask every happy customer at the "wow" moment (delivery, pickup, result)
- Send the direct review link on WhatsApp:
  `Thank you for choosing us 🙏 Could you share a quick review? It really helps a local business: [link]`
- **Reply to every review**, good and bad, within 48 hours
- Never buy fake reviews (risk of suspension + it breaks trust)

**4. Do you need a website?**
Not on day 1. A strong GBP + Instagram + WhatsApp is enough to start. Add a 1-page landing page when running ads or selling higher-ticket services.

### Live demo
Open a GBP dashboard (yours or a client's **[YOUR EXAMPLE]**) and go through the checklist live. Search "[category] near me" in Jammu, compare the top 3 results, and ask the class why they rank.

### Mini practice task (not graded)

### Quiz: Class 4
1. Three factors in local ranking:
   **Relevance, Distance, Prominence**
2. The most important GBP setting to get right:
   a) Cover photo b) **Primary category** c) Description length
3. Adding "Best Cheap Bakery Jammu" to the business name is…
   **A violation that risks suspension**
4. When should you ask for a review?
   **Right after the customer's "wow" moment**
5. Should you reply to negative reviews?
   **Yes, calmly and quickly, offering to fix it**

# CLASS 5: WhatsApp Business: Capture, Reply, Follow Up, Close
**Goal:** students set up WhatsApp Business so no lead is lost, and write a follow-up sequence.

### Run of show (90 min)
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Recap + Assignment 3 feedback |
| 10–20 | Why leads die (speed + follow-up) |
| 20–50 | Live demo: WhatsApp Business setup |
| 50–70 | Follow-up sequence + scripts |
| 70–80 | Simple lead tracking sheet |
| 80–90 | Final project briefing + Q&A |

### Teaching notes
**1. Why leads die**
- Slow reply. Reply within **5 minutes** in business hours; speed wins.
- No follow-up. Most people don't buy on the first message.
- No tracking. You forget who asked what.

**2. WhatsApp Business setup checklist**
- [ ] Business profile: category, address, hours, email, website, description
- [ ] **Catalogue:** products/services with photos + prices
- [ ] **Greeting message:** for new chats
- [ ] **Away message:** outside hours ("We'll reply by 10 AM 🙏")
- [ ] **Quick replies:** `/price`, `/location`, `/timing`, `/payment`
- [ ] **Labels:** New lead · Interested · Payment pending · Won · Follow-up
- [ ] Short link `wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX` in Instagram bio, GBP and ads
- [ ] Broadcast lists: only reach people who **saved your number**, so ask them to save it

**3. The 4-touch follow-up sequence (copy-paste)**
- **Day 0 (instant):** `Hi [Name] 👋 Thanks for reaching out to [Business]! You asked about [X]. Here are the details: [price/info]. Would you like to [book/visit] this week?`
- **Day 1:** `Hi [Name], just checking: did you get a chance to look at the details? Happy to answer any questions 🙂`
- **Day 3:** `[Proof]: here's what [a customer] said last week: "…". We have [slots/stock] for [day]. Shall I reserve one for you?`
- **Day 7:** `Hi [Name], last message from my side 🙏 If now isn't the right time, no problem. Just reply "LATER" and I'll check in next month.`

**4. Lead tracking sheet (Google Sheets)**
Columns: Date · Name · Phone · Source (Insta/Ads/Google/Referral) · Interested in · Status · Next follow-up date · Notes
"If it's not in the sheet, it didn't happen."

### Live demo
On a phone (screen mirror): set up a greeting message, an away message, 3 quick replies, labels and one catalogue item.

### Quiz: Class 5
1. Target reply time for a new lead in business hours:
   **Under 5 minutes**
2. Who receives your broadcast messages?
   **Only contacts who have saved your number**
3. What does a "quick reply" do?
   **Sends a saved message with a shortcut like /price**
4. How many follow-up touches are in our sequence?
   **4 (Day 0, 1, 3, 7)**
5. Name 3 columns of a lead sheet.
   **Any 3: Date, Name, Phone, Source, Status, Next follow-up, Notes**

### Homework

# CLASS 6: Measuring Results + Final Project Presentations
**Goal:** students read basic numbers, make decisions from them, and present their 1-page marketing plan.

### Run of show (90 min)
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–20 | Measuring what matters |
| 20–30 | Weekly review routine |
| 30–80 | Final project presentations (3–4 min each + 1 min feedback) |
| 80–90 | Wrap-up, next steps, feedback form, upsell |

### Teaching notes
**1. The scorecard (weekly)**
| Metric | Where to find it | Good sign |
|---|---|---|
| Reach / views | Instagram Insights | Growing week on week |
| Non-follower % | Insights | High = content reaching new people |
| Profile visits → link/WhatsApp taps | Insights | Taps rising |
| Leads | Lead sheet | Steady or rising |
| CPL | Ads Manager | Stable or falling |
| Leads → customers (conversion %) | Lead sheet | Know this number |

**2. Decision rules**
- High reach, low profile visits → content isn't creating interest; improve hooks and CTAs
- Many profile visits, few WhatsApp taps → bio/offer problem
- Many leads, few sales → follow-up/offer/price problem, not a marketing problem
- CPL rising → new creative first, then audience

**3. Weekly review routine (30 min, every Monday)**
1. Fill the scorecard
2. What worked best last week? (top post/ad)
3. What to stop?
4. One test for this week
5. Plan the next 7 posts

### Final presentations
Each student presents Assignment 4. Feedback format: **1 thing that's strong · 1 thing to fix · 1 idea to test.**

### Wrap-up (say this)
- Completion requirement reminder (all classes + 4 assignments)
- Next step: **Meta Ads in Practice** for students who want to go deeper on ads (offer an alumni discount if you decide to)
- Feedback form + ask for a review/testimonial (screenshot or 30-sec video), which you need for course marketing

### Quiz: Class 6 (final)
1. Lots of leads but few sales usually means a problem with:
   **Follow-up, offer or price**
2. High reach but few profile visits means:
   **Weak hooks/CTA; content isn't creating interest**
3. What should you change first when CPL rises?
   **The creative**
4. How often should you do a review?
   **Weekly**
5. Formula for conversion %:
   **Customers ÷ Leads × 100**
