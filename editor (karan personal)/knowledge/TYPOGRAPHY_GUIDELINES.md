# Typography Guidelines

Font names below are **visual matches, UNVERIFIED**. Replace with real names when Karan confirms or supplies project files.

## Style A — Clean Tech Minimal
| Role | Look observed | Suggested match (UNVERIFIED) | Case | Size @1080×1920 (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook title | Regular sans, white on black box | SF Pro Display / Inter Regular | Sentence case | 60–70 px |
| Spoken captions | Thin–regular neo-grotesk, white, soft shadow | Inter Light/Regular, SF Pro | As spoken (lowercase mid-sentence) | 90–110 px |
| Emphasis word | Same family, Bold | Inter Bold | — | same |
| Card headings | Black, regular/medium | Inter Medium | Title case | 50–60 px |
| CTA "Comment" label | Small regular, white | Inter Regular | Title case | ~35 px |
| CTA keyword | Medium/semibold black in quotes | Inter Semibold | Title case | ~45 px |

## Style B — Bold Indian Creator
| Role | Look observed | Suggested match (UNVERIFIED) | Case | Size (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook title | Bold rounded geometric sans | Poppins / Nunito ExtraBold | Title case | 55–65 px |
| Captions | Bold sans, white + dark outline | Montserrat ExtraBold | ALL CAPS | 40–50 px |
| Emphasis captions | Condensed bold, yellow/gold | Bebas Neue / Anton | ALL CAPS | 80–100 px |
| CTA "Comment" | Script | Great Vibes–style script | — | ~80 px |

## Rules
- Max 2 font families per project.
- Captions 1–3 words per beat; never a full paragraph on screen.
- Brand names spelled correctly (ChatGPT, YouTube, WhatsApp).
