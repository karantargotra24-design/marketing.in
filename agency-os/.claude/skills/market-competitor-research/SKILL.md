# market-competitor-research

**Purpose:** Industry and competitor research with sources.

**Required inputs:** workspace, niche, city/region

**Workflow:** 1 Define scope. 2 WebSearch 3-5 competitors. 3 Capture offers, pricing (if public), proof, content. 4 Separate facts vs assumptions.

**Output template:** research/competitors-<date>.md

**Quality checklist:** Every claim has URL+date; limits stated. Plus rules in knowledge/00 (sources, no invented data, isolation).

**Example invocation:** /research client-001
