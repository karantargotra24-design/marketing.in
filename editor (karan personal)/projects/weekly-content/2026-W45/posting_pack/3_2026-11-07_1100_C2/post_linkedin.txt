Leads come in. Follow-ups don't happen. For small businesses, that's the cheapest leak to fix.

My simple 3-message system:
1. Right away: thanks + one question
2. Next day: one useful answer + an offer to call
3. Day three: a soft close

Rules: use their name, one question per message, stop after three, and only message opted-in leads.
