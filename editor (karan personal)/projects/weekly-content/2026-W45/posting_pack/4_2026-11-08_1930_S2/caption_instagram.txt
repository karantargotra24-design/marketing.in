How to test an AI tool before you pay for it.

Every week there's a new 'must-have' AI tool. Before you take out your card, run a 20-minute test:

1. Pick one real task from your week
2. Run the same prompt in 2 tools
3. Time how long it takes to get a usable result
4. Check every fact it gives you

The tool that wins on your own task is the one worth paying for. Not the one with the best ads.
