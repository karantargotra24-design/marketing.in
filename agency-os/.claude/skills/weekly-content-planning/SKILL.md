# weekly-content-planning

**Purpose:** Plan one week of content with full item specs.

**Required inputs:** workspace, week dates, pillars, goals

**Workflow:** 1 Read profile, research, last 2 weeks content (no repeats). 2 Plan items: date, platform, format, pillar, objective, hook, copy, caption, CTA, visual direction, KPI. 3 Add approval checklist.

**Output template:** content/week-<date>.md

**Quality checklist:** No repetition; formats allowed by H6; preview date = 1 day before. Plus rules in knowledge/00 (sources, no invented data, isolation).

**Example invocation:** /content-week personal
