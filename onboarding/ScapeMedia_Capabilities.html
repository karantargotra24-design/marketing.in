About ScapeMedia

A performance marketing partner for businesses that measure success in leads and sales, not likes.

ScapeMedia is a Jammu-based agency specialising in Meta advertising. We plan, run and optimise Facebook and Instagram campaigns around one question: is the spend bringing in qualified leads that turn into customers? Every decision we make is guided by data, tested in the market and reported in plain language.
