# social-360-audit

**Purpose:** Audit profile, content, engagement, brand, conversion, discoverability.

**Required inputs:** handles/URLs actually accessible

**Workflow:** 1 Inventory assets. 2 Mark inaccessible NOT INSPECTED. 3 Evaluate each area. 4 Prioritise by impact/confidence/cost/effort.

**Output template:** audits/audit-<date>.md

**Quality checklist:** No claims about inaccessible accounts; evidence per problem. Plus rules in knowledge/00 (sources, no invented data, isolation).

**Example invocation:** /audit client-000-TEST
