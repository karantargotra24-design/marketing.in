## C2 — Saved replies + ChatGPT (How-to)

| # | Copy (red part in **bold**) | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | AUTOMATION · Answering the same DM daily? **Do it in 1 tap.** · Instagram saved replies + ChatGPT. Free, works on your phone. | Phone outline + chat bubble |
| 2 | Price? Location? Timings? **On repeat.** · If your DMs keep asking the same 5-6 questions, typing each reply by hand eats your week. | 3 repeating bubbles |
| 3 | **Step 1** · List your top 5 DM questions · Scroll your last 30 DMs. Write down what repeats: price, location, timings, delivery, booking. | Numbered list |
| 4 | **Step 2** · Draft replies with ChatGPT · Prompt: 'Write 5 short, friendly DM replies for my [business] answering: [questions]. Under 40 words each, end with one next step.' | Prompt box, [brackets] red |
| 5 | **Step 3** · Save them in Instagram · Settings → Business tools → Saved replies → +. Add a shortcut like 'price' and paste the reply. Menu names vary by version. | Breadcrumb with red → |
| 6 | **Step 4** · Type the shortcut, tap, send · In any DM, type 'price' and tap the suggestion. Edit one detail so it still feels personal. | Chat bar mock |
| 7 | **Save this for setup day** · Needs a Business or Creator account. Start with your top 3 questions. | Red Save button |

**Caption**
> Instagram saved replies: same DM ka jawab 1 tap mein. Har din 'price kya hai?', 'location kahan hai?' type karte ho? Yeh 15 minute ka setup hai.
>
> 1. Last 30 DMs dekho, jo questions repeat hote hain unki list banao
> 2. ChatGPT se 5 short replies likhwao (prompt slide 4 pe hai)
> 3. Instagram mein Saved replies mein daalo, har ek ka shortcut rakho jaise 'price'
> 4. DM mein shortcut type karo, tap karo, ek detail personalise karo, send
>
> Bas dhyan rakho: reply mein price/timings badle toh saved reply bhi update karo. Galat info wala fast reply, slow reply se bura hai.
>
> Note: yeh feature Business ya Creator account pe milta hai. Menu ka naam app version ke hisaab se thoda alag ho sakta hai.
>
> Save kar lo — setup karte waqt kaam aayega.

## S2 — Ads like a WhatsApp message (Do/Don't)

- **Tag:** DIGITAL MARKETING
- **Headline:** Write ads like a WhatsApp message **not a pamphlet**
- **Support:** Do: talk to one person, show the ₹ price, give one next step. Don't: 'best quality', 5 offers, no price.
- **CTA:** Send this to someone running ads
- **Visual:** Split Do / Don't card, red ticks on Do, grey strikethrough on Don't.

**Caption**
> Ad copy ko WhatsApp message ki tarah likho, pamphlet ki tarah nahi.
>
> Pamphlet wali copy: "Best quality, lowest price, all services available, contact now!" Isse koi rukta nahi.
>
> WhatsApp wali copy: "Ghar ka AC service ₹499 mein, Jammu city mein same-day visit. Slot book karne ke liye 'AC' likh ke bhejo."
>
> Farak dekho:
> ✔ Ek insaan se baat
> ✔ ₹ price saaf
> ✔ Ek hi next step
>
> Agli ad likhne se pehle zor se padho. Agar dost ko aise message nahi bhejoge, toh customer ko bhi mat bhejo.
>
> Us dost ko bhejo jo apni shop ki ads khud chalata hai.
