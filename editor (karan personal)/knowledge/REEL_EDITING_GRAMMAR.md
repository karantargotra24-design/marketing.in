# Reel Editing Grammar: how the reference reels are cut (analysed 31 Oct 2026)

Method: scene detection at 0.12 (catches soft cuts), a start + end frame of every shot (shows zooms and builds), EBU R128 loudness, silence scan. Shot strips: `references/videos/shot_strips/strip1–4.jpg`.
Not verifiable from the files: exact SFX and music tracks (no stems), caption fonts. Marked as observations only.

## The 9 rules
1. **Hook = face + title in the first second.** The title stays on screen through the whole opening line (ref 01 keeps its black title box ~8 s). The first cut comes after the spoken hook (0.9–2.1 s).
2. **Second beat = a "map" graphic.** Right after the hook, one graphic shows what's coming: a topic map, a logo cloud or a name card (ref 03 at 0.9 s, ref 02 at 2.1 s).
3. **A-roll is never static.** Every A-roll cut alternates **wide ↔ punch-in (about 115–130%)**, and some shots have a slow push-in or pull-out inside the shot.
4. **Captions never stop.** 1–3 words, word by word, running over A-roll, B-roll and graphics alike. Over white graphics they turn black. Captions alone create rhythm even in a long A-roll shot.
5. **Graphics are built, not shown.** White-canvas motion graphics where elements appear **on the spoken word**: logos pop, lines draw, list items appear one by one, a highlight sweeps across the key line. A graphic shot can hold 5–11 s because it keeps building.
6. **Overlays on A-roll.** Icons, tiles or a small card pop in **over the presenter** (ref 03: icons above the head, a doc icon in front, 4 system tiles in a row). Not every visual needs a full cutaway.
7. **One burst montage per reel.** 4–6 very short shots (0.2–1.2 s), photos, B-roll or labelled cards, cut fast to speed up the middle (ref 01 at 21–24 s, ref 02 at 31–36 s).
8. **Transitions = mostly hard cuts.** Graphic-to-graphic changes use *morphs* (a tile grows or moves to the next layout) or a quick blur. Colour-wash flashes (0.2 s) only in the Bold/Hinglish style (ref 04), not in Style A.
9. **End on the face, not on a card.** The last 4–6 s stay on the presenter talking, with the white "Comment" pill animating in over them (all four refs).

## Karan's version (Style A, his colours)
Hook box black/white · captions thin white (black on white cards), one bold word · graphics on white with red as the only accent · logo tiles in official colours · music bed at about −25 dB under the voice · master −14 LUFS.
