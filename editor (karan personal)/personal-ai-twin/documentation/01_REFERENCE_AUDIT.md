## Why AI images can't be the source of truth
- **They drift.** Images 1–3 and the video already disagree with each other and with the real rooftop photo on hair volume, beard density, face width and apparent age. Training a twin on them locks in an AI person, not you.
- **The voice isn't yours.** Cloning from file 4 would clone Gemini's voice.
- **It breaks your brand rule.** Design System v2 says never present an AI likeness of Karan as real.
