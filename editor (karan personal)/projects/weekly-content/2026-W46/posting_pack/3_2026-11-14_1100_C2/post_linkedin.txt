Clicks but no leads is rarely a budget problem.

Before scaling, check what happens after the click:
1. Is the offer clear at first glance on mobile?
2. Are you asking for more form fields than you need?
3. How fast does a new lead get a reply?
4. Does the offer say what they get, for whom, and the price range?

Fix the leak, then scale.
