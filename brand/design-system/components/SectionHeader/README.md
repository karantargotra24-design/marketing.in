# SectionHeader

The opening of every section: red eyebrow with a short rule, a serif headline with one red punch word, and an optional lead paragraph.

- Headline: Instrument Serif; the punch word in Plus Jakarta Sans 800 caps, accent colour. One per headline.
- Eyebrow: 2 to 5 words, uppercase.
- Lead: one or two sentences in ink-muted, max 56 characters per line.
