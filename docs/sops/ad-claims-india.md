# SOP: Claims to check before any ad or post (general guidance, not legal advice)
- Scarcity ("only 8 left"), discounts vs struck-through MRP, star ratings and testimonials must be real, current and provable. Placeholder/"Sample" reviews must never be shown as real.
- Food and wellness products: show the FSSAI licence number; avoid disease/cure claims; get label claims reviewed.
- Real estate: no returns/appreciation promises; RERA registration number where required; lawyer review for legal statements.
- Using these rules does not replace a lawyer or platform policy review.
